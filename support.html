Frequently asked questions

How do I reset all of my data?

Open the app, go to Settings → Reset / Clear All Data, and confirm. This wipes journal entries, photos, trainer scores, and timer state. The action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Where is my journal data stored?

All journal entries, photos, and trainer progress are stored locally in the app's sandbox on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Uninstalling the app removes the data with it.

Can I use the app without an internet connection?

Yes. Poker Lab is fully offline. Every tool — including the equity simulator and tournament clock — works without any network connection.

The app is asking for camera / photo access. Is that required?

Only if you want to attach a photo to a journal entry. You can deny the permission and continue using every other feature. Permissions can be changed anytime in iOS Settings.

How do I attach a photo to a journal entry?
  1. Open a journal entry, or create a new one from the Journal tab.
  2. Tap the photo area and choose Take Photo or Choose from Library.
  3. Grant the requested permission the first time. You can revoke it later in iOS Settings.
  4. The image is saved with the entry on your device only.
How accurate is the equity compare tool?

Equity Compare runs a fast Monte Carlo simulation against random opponent hands (or two hands of your choosing). It is a study aid, not a guarantee. Higher iteration counts give more stable numbers; the default is tuned for quick, useful results on a phone.

How do I change the tournament clock level length?

Open the Tournament Clock tab, tap the structure name (Standard / Turbo / Deep Stack), then adjust the level length with the stepper. The new length applies to the current and all following levels.

The sound on the tournament clock is too quiet (or too loud)

Use the in-app volume toggle in the Tournament Clock header, or adjust your device's media volume. The in-app setting is independent of the ringer/silent switch.

Does Poker Lab have ads, in-app purchases, or subscriptions?

No. The app is a single, paid (or free, depending on your store listing) download with no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscriptions related to gameplay.

The app crashes or behaves unexpectedly. What should I do?
  1. Force-quit and reopen the app.
  2. Make sure your iOS version is up to date (Settings → General → Software Update).
  3. Reinstall the app (your local data will be lost — back up journal entries first if needed).
  4. If the issue persists, email litzybuziemextn527@gmail.com with a description and screenshot.
How do I request a feature or suggest an improvement?

We'd love to hear it. Send your idea to litzybuziemextn527@gmail.com with the subject line "Feature Request". A short description and a use case help us prioritize.
