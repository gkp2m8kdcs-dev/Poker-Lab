Everything you need to study the game

Eight purpose-built tools. One calm, dark interface. No ads, no sign-ups, no data collection.

🃏

Hand Strength

Score any 5-to-7 card combination. See the best 5-card hand, its rank, and rarity at a glance.

⚖️

Equity Compare

Fast Monte Carlo simulation that estimates the win percentage of two hands against an optional board.

🎯

Outs & Draws

Count the cards that improve your hand on the turn and river, with an approximate hit rate.

💰

Pot Odds

Compute the equity you need to call a bet of any size — the foundation of every +EV decision.

📚

Hand Trainer

Drill preflop decisions: identify hands, count outs, rank boards, play or fold in position. Streak & accuracy saved locally.

⏱️

Tournament Clock

Three blind ladders — Standard, Turbo, Deep Stack. Adjustable level lengths, last-10-seconds audio ticks, pause/resume.

📓

Journal

Save practice hands: position, hole cards, board, result, pot, notes, and an optional photo. Searchable and filterable.

📊

Reference Charts

169 starting hand chart, full hand rankings, and a betting rounds reference — all one tap away.

Frequently asked questions

Does Poker Lab involve real-money gambling?

No. Poker Lab is an educational tool only. There are no bets, no wagering, no jackpots, and no in-app purchases related to gameplay.

Does Poker Lab collect any data about me?

No. The app does not include analytics or tracking SDKs, does not contact any server for gameplay data, and does not sell or share anything. See the Privacy Policy.

What information do I need to provide to use the app?

None. You can open Poker Lab and use every tool without signing up, creating an account, or sharing contact details.

Where is my journal data stored?

All journal entries, photos, scores, and timer state are stored locally on your device. You can clear everything in Settings.

How do I get support?

Email us at litzybuziemextn527@gmail.com. Visit the Support page for full details.

Is Poker Lab suitable for beginners?

Yes. The hand rankings chart, pot odds reference, and preflop trainer are designed to help new players build a solid foundation.
