Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Poker Lab ("we", "our", "the app") is an offline Texas Hold'em study companion. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app handles — and, just as importantly, what it does not.

Short version: Poker Lab does not collect personal data. It does not include analytics, ad networks, or third-party tracking SDKs. All journal entries, photos, scores, and timer state are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers because we do not operate any gameplay servers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, address, ID numbers).
  • Account credentials — the app has no sign-up or login.
  • Usage analytics, device fingerprinting, or advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID).
  • Location data, contacts, calendars, or background activity.
  • Crash reports — the app does not include a crash-reporting SDK.
  • Any data about your poker hands, session results, or training history.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To deliver its features, Poker Lab stores the following on your device only:

  • Journal entries — position, hole cards, board, result, pot size, notes, and timestamps you enter.
  • Journal photos — optional images you attach from the camera or photo library.
  • Trainer progress — accuracy, streak, and counters for the hand trainer drills.
  • Tournament timer state — current structure, level, and remaining time.
  • App preferences — sound on/off, theme, and other UI settings.

All of this data lives in the app's local sandbox. It is never transmitted off your device by the app. Deleting the app removes this data.

3. Permissions Requested by the App

Poker Lab requests only the iOS permissions it needs to function, and only at the moment they are needed:

  • Camera — so you can photograph a hand or table state to attach to a journal entry. The image is saved locally; nothing is uploaded.
  • Photo Library (read) — so you can attach an existing image to a journal entry.
  • Photo Library (add) — only if you choose to save a hand summary snapshot to your library.

You can deny any of these permissions and continue to use the rest of the app. Permissions can be changed anytime in iOS Settings.

4. Network Activity

The app does not require a network connection for any feature. If your device is offline, every tool — including the Monte Carlo equity simulation, the tournament clock, and the journal — works exactly the same. The app does not make background network calls.

5. Children

Poker Lab is an educational tool. It does not target children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, this is enforced by design.

6. Your Rights and Controls

Because the app stores data only on your device, you are always in control:

  • Access & export — view, copy, or back up your journal entries from inside the app.
  • Delete individual records — swipe-to-delete on journal entries and trainer records.
  • Delete everything — Settings offers a "Reset / Clear All Data" action that wipes all locally stored data in one tap.
  • Uninstall — removing the app deletes its local sandbox, including all journal entries and photos.

7. Third-Party Services

Poker Lab does not embed any third-party analytics, advertising, social, or crash-reporting SDKs. There are no third parties that receive your data through the app.

8. International Use

Poker Lab is developed in the United States and is intended for a global audience. Because no personal data is collected, transmitted, or stored on our side, regional data-protection laws (including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and similar frameworks) impose no collection obligations on us. If you have a privacy question, contact us and we will respond.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, surface a notice inside the app. Because we do not collect contact information, we cannot notify you by email — please review this page when you update the app.
